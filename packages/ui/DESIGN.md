# Design System: Fieldtrip UI

The frontmatter above is generated from `theme.css` and `fieldtrip.css` and is the normative token list. This prose explains why the system looks the way it does; it names tokens and never restates their values. The rules for using the kit (which component, variant, page pattern and copy) live in [`packages/standards/skills/ft-ui/SKILL.md`](../standards/skills/ft-ui/SKILL.md), and `ft check` enforces them.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Shared Workbench"**

Fieldtrip UI is the plumbing every Fieldtrip app shares: shell, page frame, tables, forms, dialogs, states. It is meant to disappear into the work. Staff spend long stretches in these apps and creators drop in from a link, so the interface stays calm, legible and predictable, and the product's own identity (its signature surfaces, its look) carries the character.

The canvas is a warm off-white, not a stark white, and the kit is light only, with no dark theme. It needs room to breathe: generous padding, a 13px floor for meta text, and type that never shrinks to fit. Hierarchy comes from tone, weight and spacing before it comes from lines or colour.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm, low-chroma neutrals on hue 95; colour is reserved for status and one highlight surface.
- Surfaces separate by tone, not by borders.
- Every value is a role token, so a scale change happens once in `theme.css`.
- Three looks share the same components: neutral, Fieldtrip brand, product-specific.

## Colors

A warm grey ramp carries the interface; colour means something when it appears.

### Primary

- **Ink** (`primary`, `foreground`): near-black warm grey. Primary buttons, body text and the caret. Under the Fieldtrip look it becomes `fieldtrip-ink`.

### Neutral

- **Warm Canvas** (`background`): the page.
- **Paper White** (`card`, `popover`): cards, menus and popovers sit white on the canvas.
- **Sidebar Shade** (`sidebar`): one step deeper than the canvas, so the shell separates without a line.
- **Quiet Fills** (`secondary`, `muted`, `accent`): secondary buttons, hover and selected rows.
- **Faint Edges** (`border`, `input`): only where spacing cannot group things.
- **Text levels** (`foreground`, `muted-foreground`, `subtle-foreground`): primary, secondary and meta. Each passes WCAG AA on cards, canvas and sidebar; subtle is the floor.

### Status

- **Success, Warning, Info, Destructive**: Fieldtrip's status hues for every app, so "Pending" or "Approved" reads the same everywhere. The base token marks dots and icons; its `-foreground` pair is readable text in that colour. Toned surfaces derive tint, ink and solid from `--tone` at fixed lightnesses per theme (`theme.test.ts` checks AA and hue).

### Highlight

- **Highlight** (`highlight`): the one coloured surface a page may use, for its hero figure. Powder blue under the Fieldtrip look. Also the text selection colour.

### Fieldtrip brand palette

- `fieldtrip-*` and `fieldtrip-*-light` pairs (ink, olive, orange, mauve, brown, green, spring, yellow, sky, pink, powder) from the brand Figma palette. Available only when an app imports `fieldtrip.css`; for brand moments and signature surfaces, not for plumbing.
### Named Rules

**The Earned Colour Rule.** Interface chrome stays neutral. Colour appears as status, as the one highlight surface, or inside a product's signature surface.

## Typography

**Interface Font:** Geist (with system sans fallback), for all interface text.
**Statement Font:** Diplomat (`font-serif`, Fieldtrip look only), for sparing statements on a heading or page role.
**Brand Font:** SwissNow (`font-brand`, Fieldtrip look only), for a wordmark or brand headline.

**Character:** a neutral, precise grotesk does the work; the serif appears rarely enough to mean something.

### Hierarchy

- **Interface roles** (`3xs` to `stat-sm`): fixed sizes, because controls and dense rows must not grow with the window. `table` for cells, `sm` for body and controls, `item` for a list row's title, `base` for reading text, `heading` for card, dialog, sheet and section titles, `lead` under a hero.
- **Page roles** (`title-sm`, `title`, `stat`, `display`): fluid from a 360px to a 1280px window, each capped at 2.5x its floor so browser zoom still reaches 200% (WCAG 1.4.4).

### Named Rules

**The Role Not Size Rule.** Text picks a role, never a pixel size. Each role carries its line height and tracking, and the heading roles their weight, so nothing adds `font-semibold` or `leading-*` on top.

## Layout

Layout components own spacing; nothing writes padding, margin or gap by hand. `Page`, `PageSection`, `Card` and `Item` pad themselves, and `Stack`, `Inline` and `Grid` space their children by role: `gap-xs` a label and its hint, `gap-sm` an icon and its text, `gap-md` items of a list, `gap-lg` blocks inside a card or form, `gap-xl` groups of blocks. An app changes its density once, by overriding the gap and padding tokens in its `globals.css`.

Every single-line control takes its height from the `control-*` tokens, so a row lines up by construction. The topbar row is `control-sm`.

Every page is `Page` > `PageHeader` > content, inside `AppShell`. List, detail, form and settings patterns are in the ft-ui skill and Storybook "Layout/Page".

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat, layered by tone: canvas, then a deeper sidebar, then white cards and popovers. Shadows are quiet and structural, never decorative.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Hairline** (`shadow-hairline`): the faintest lift, for a surface that only needs to separate from the canvas.
- **Raised** (`shadow-raised`): a resting card or control with a 1px ring.
- **Subtle** (`shadow-subtle`): a hovered or lifted element.
- **Elevated** (`shadow-elevated`): menus, popovers and dialogs above the page.

Values live in `theme.css` (`@theme inline`); the DESIGN.md token schema has no shadow group.

### Named Rules

**The Lines Only When Needed Rule.** No rules between rows, sections or label/value pairs. A list that scrolls under a header sits in a frame, so the frame is the edge rows pass behind.

## Shapes

Soft, consistent corners from one `--radius`: the `rounded` scale steps down for small controls and up for large containers. Single-line controls (buttons, badges, chips, tabs, avatars, toolbar fields) are fully rounded pills; fields in a form or dialog take the smaller field radius. The exact role-to-radius mapping is in the ft-ui skill.

Focus is one indicator everywhere: a solid 2px ring with a 2px gap (`focus-ring`), inset for rows and menu items in clipped containers.

## Components

The catalog is `src/components`, shown in Storybook (atoms, molecules, organisms and the page patterns in Organisms/Page). The component and pattern stories are the regression baseline. Which component, variant and pattern to use is decided in the ft-ui skill, not here.

- **Buttons:** fully rounded at the `control-*` heights; `default` (ink) is the one primary action, the rest `secondary`, `outline`, `ghost` or an icon. `destructive` only confirms a delete.
- **Cards:** white on the canvas, `rounded-xl`, padded by `card-padding`. The container for any custom content. `CardTitle` is an `h2`; inside a titled `PageSection` it takes `level={3}`.
- **Inputs:** field radius in forms and dialogs, pill inside a `PageToolbar`; `input` border, focus ring on focus.
- **Navigation:** `AppShell` + `AppNav`; the shell shows the app's name only.
- **Tables:** `DataTableShell` + `DataTable` in a framed container; people appear as avatar stacks.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** change a token in `theme.css` or `fieldtrip.css`, then run `bun run design` in `packages/ui`; the test fails until DESIGN.md matches.
- **Do** give a product its look through token overrides in the app's `globals.css`, never through classes on kit components.
- **Do** judge a kit change on the real app screens it lands in, not on an isolated story.

### Don't:

- **Don't** hand-set colours, spacing lengths or type sizes; `ft check` fails on them.
- **Don't** copy, fork or restyle a kit component in an app; compose the gap and mark it `// toolkit-gap:`.
- **Don't** add a second coloured surface to a page; `highlight` is the one.
- **Don't** separate things with lines where spacing can group them.
